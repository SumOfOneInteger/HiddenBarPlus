## Privacy Policy

Hidden Bar Plus is an open source app provided by SumOfOneInteger at no cost and is
intended for use as is.

**Information Collection and Use**

The app does not collect, store or transmit any information that identifies you.

Its only network activity is the update check: Hidden Bar Plus periodically
downloads a small XML file (the Sparkle "appcast") from the update server named in
the app to learn whether a newer version exists, and downloads that version if you
accept it. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to the server that
hosts the appcast. Hidden Bar Plus does not send system information with that
request. You can decline automatic update checks when the app asks, and change
your choice later in Preferences.

The app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

**Contact Us**

Questions or suggestions about this policy: open an issue at
